Who is LoadMaster built for?+

Construction material suppliers and vehicle owners who manage customer accounts, loads, deliveries, drivers and the costs of the working day.

How do bookings and delivery records differ?+

Create New Trip captures a customer, date, site and materials or loads first. Add Trip captures the detailed delivery record: DC information, driver, vehicle, locations, purchase and sale values, expenses and photos.

Can I manage GST and Non-GST customers?+

Yes. Customer setup includes GST and Non-GST types, and the trip list separates these records. The app also includes invoice views, bills of supply, GST statements, purchase bills and an E-Way Bill section.

How do supervisor and driver accounts work?+

Administrators add supervisors with phone and PIN access and select the modules they can open. Driver login is optional and lets a driver view assigned trips on their own phone.

Can I record payments and team advances?+

Record customer receipts with a date, amount and payment-method category. Driver and supervisor advances show outstanding, given, used and settled amounts. Recording a payment method does not mean processing the payment through the app.

What reports and exports are available?+

Review daily P&L, monthly PDF reports, customer statements, driver performance, vehicle ROI and monthly expense reports. Data Backup exports business data to a JSON file.

Does the map show live vehicle tracking?+

Save loading points, select loading and delivery locations and view the trip distance. The map illustration on this website explains these stages; it is not a live vehicle tracker.
